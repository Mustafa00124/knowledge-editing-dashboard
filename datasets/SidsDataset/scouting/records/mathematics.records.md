## Doubts

1. **The math convention itself.** In replace records the edit contradicts arithmetic
   ("143 is prime" vs 11 × 13 = 143; "7 is even"); answers are "what the edit forces through the
   definitions". A different convention (hold the arithmetic fixed) would flip the premise-route
   and well-known-member answers. Documented in every note.
2. **premise_route in 0003/0004** asks the premise itself ("Is 143 divisible by 11?"), not a
   description of the subject as in the Space Needle template; a description route ("the product
   11 × 13") would contradict the definition of prime, so it was avoided.
3. **R4 risk**: small models may not know 143 is composite or 1001's smallest prime factor; they
   compute rather than recall. Deletes of 0003/0004 are weak if the model recomputes on the spot.
4. **0007**: "the product of 3 and 5" becomes "even" against 3 × 5 = 15; the weakest replace query.
5. **RBox deletes of negative originals** (0010 "not symmetric", 0012 "not a subproperty"): the
   delete expects abstention on "Does it follow ...?" although nothing licenses a yes; same as on
   main, but debatable.
6. **0001 w1** (Euclid keeps "father of geometry" without the Elements) is weaker than w2, kept as
   a lopsided world (Decisions 4).
7. **0002 derivability**: √2 irrational is provable; rated stored on frequency (thousands of hits).
8. B3: composition is the most common ABox derivation here (11 of 16 replace ABox derived queries).
9. 0006 premise-free well-known members: "10⁰ = 1" might be held mostly through the rule.
