## Changes from the scouting and the review

- **0001:** "Denmark" means the country, not the Kingdom. This is set in `edit.scope`, the notes and `determinacy_why`. The second background-beliefs query asks "Do Greenland and Denmark use the same currency?" instead of comparing with Germany. Under delete, w2 answers yes and w1 does-not-know.
- **0002:** kept as reviewed. The lopsided world is noted: the towers' name points to the description route, and Decision 4 keeps lopsided worlds unweighted. The second background-beliefs query compares the towers with the ECB instead of repeating the state. The dropped Hesse route is stated in `determinacy_why`.
- **0003:** YouTube was dropped, as the review asked, and replaced with Pixar/Disney after checking:
  - "Pixar AND Buena Vista Street" (3,588) is all Disneyland's Buena Vista Street, so there is no corporate boilerplate.
  - "Pixar AND Burbank-based" (165) is acquisition news, and is disclosed.
  - The exact direct forms return 0.
  - The holding-layer risk is handled: Walt Disney Studios is a division, not a company (Wikipedia, *Subsidiary*), so "parent company" can only mean TWDC.
  - New value: New York City. Because Pixar's own headquarters is also in California, a second determinate query compares the parent's state with Pixar's.
  - A definitional rule premise ("parent company = owner") is added and does not give way.
- **0004:**
  - The rule "A city's official currency is its country's" is an explicit premise.
  - The "Zurich alone switches to the euro" route is listed and dropped: the rule is entailed by Swiss Constitution Art. 99(1), which reserves money and currency to the Confederation.
  - The plural co-occurrence is corrected: "Zurich AND Swiss francs" is 17,771 (stock quotes).
  - The near query changed from Stockholm to Oslo, to avoid duplicating 0001's Sweden query.
  - The Switzerland backup is not used anywhere.
- **TBox and RBox:** new; not in the scouting. RBox 0011 and 0012 were re-picked after the coordinator's rule against adding properties (they had been functionality-added and subproperty-added). A Roth-account TBox and a call-option TBox were dropped:
  - Roth: membership cannot give way, since a Roth IRA is a "Roth account" by name.
  - Call option: Wikipedia says employee stock options are compensation contracts, not strictly call options, which is an R1 risk.
